The subject, up close —

Twelve frames, zero direction. Frame 01 is a product designer from New Delhi agreeing to “just one photo.” By frame 04 an opinion is forming; by frame 05 it has won. Frame 07 is the keeper — marked in red, non-negotiable. And by frame 12 he is art-directing the shoot he’s in: warmer light, tighter crop, fire the photographer (himself). None of this was posed, which is rather the point. Up close, the face works like the designer — every expression is a draft, and he ships all twelve.
