Philosophy

Three rules I design by.

the whole brief 128
Philosophy
  • 01.
    The empty state earns the page.

    It's the first real screen a new user meets, so it has to orient them and offer the first move. I write it before the dashboard it leads to.

  • 02.
    Error copy is the apology.

    When something breaks, the message owes two things in plain words: whether we know what happened, and what they do next. Never "something went wrong."

  • 03.
    Design for the 2am edge case.

    Tired user, one bar of signal, a half-finished form on the third try. Hold for them and the happy path was never in question.
