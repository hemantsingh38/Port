About me

He was always an artist.
Now he builds things that
feel like memories.

Hemant Singh is a UX designer with 3.5 years across ed tech, fintech, e-commerce and B2B SaaS. He founded Early Winter as a small, slow, intentional studio — one person, a lot of care.

The name is the brief: work that feels like the first cold morning of the season. Present, calm, and a little nostalgic.

Before the pixels there were sketchbooks, and before the sketchbooks there was a kid who drew on everything. The medium changed; the instinct didn't.

iii / viii — my philosophy
Philosophy

Design should feel like the first cold morning of the season.

— the whole philosophy, honestly 128
Three rules
  • 01.
    Invisible until it matters.

    Good design disappears into use. If someone notices the interface, it usually means something went wrong.

  • 02.
    Less, but warmer.

    Rams was right about less. But minimal shouldn't mean cold — restraint and warmth can live on the same screen.

  • 03.
    Made for Tuesdays.

    Designed to be used on an ordinary Tuesday, and remembered on a slow Sunday. Everyday first, memorable second.

    films that feel like weather. Slow ones. The kind you remember as a temperature.
