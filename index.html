About

He was always
an artist.

Hemant Singh is a UX designer with 3.5 years across ed tech, fintech, e-commerce and B2B SaaS. Based in New Delhi, he founded Early Winter as a small, slow, intentional studio. His work is shaped by Don Norman, by cinema, and by the belief that good design should feel like the first cold morning of the season — present, calm, and a little nostalgic. Currently open to new work and collaborations.
